Better Music

by rocketkid300 · v1.0.0 beta 1 · iOS 15 · Dopamine (rootless)

Beta. Gives the Apple Music app on iOS 15 the look and feel of the iOS 26 version: tinted album pages, glass buttons, word-by-word lyrics and an expandable lock screen player.

Features

  • Album pages: the whole page is tinted with the cover's colour; round Shuffle, wide Play pill, glass back button
  • Lyrics: the current line fills in word by word, in sync with the song
  • Lock screen: tap the album cover to expand it — big cover, colour backdrop, clock row and glass controls
  • Mini player: floating glass capsule with a clean pull-down animation
  • Home / New tab names, rounder covers and cards, profile circle colours
  • App size slider, with a default and a reset button
  • Safe mode that switches the visual changes off by itself if Music keeps closing

Settings

Settings → Better Music. Every option is in one place.

Requirements

  • iOS 15.x, jailbroken with Dopamine (rootless)
  • Tested on an iPhone 7 Plus running iOS 15.8.3. Other devices should work but are untested.

Known conflicts

Tweaks that also restyle the Music app or the lock screen player can fight with this one: ArtFull, Reo, Arkita. Turn them off if something looks wrong.

Privacy

The expanded lock screen cover asks Apple's public catalog (itunes.apple.com) for a full-size version of the cover art. Nothing else leaves your device, and there is no analytics.
